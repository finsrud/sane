**6. Fixed terms that are not variables**

These are set in the documents and should be changed only deliberately: the 25% outstanding threshold for the Series Seed Director seat and the protective provisions; the 10-day notice for waiving a Deemed Liquidation Event; the 90/120/150-day asset-sale redemption timeline; the 15-day and 5-day participation right periods; the 45/15/10-day right of first refusal periods; the 180-day market stand-off; the $50,000 Material Agreement threshold and the $25,000 / $100,000 liability thresholds in the representations; the $5,000 cap on Purchaser counsel fees for the next financing; the 1% threshold for joinder of new stockholders; and the 120-day and 45-day financial statement deadlines.
