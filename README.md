# Series Sane

**Series Seed (SANE Edition): Simple Agreement for Now Equity**

You've outgrown the SAFE. You're not ready for a full NVCA round. Series Sane sits in the middle: real preferred equity, a clean cap table, and a working board, without the hundred-page overhead.

---

## The problem

Seed-stage founders raising capital face an uncomfortable choice: use a SAFE and defer many of the legal decisions that matter, or negotiate a full NVCA equity round and spend a month and $50K+ in legal fees while you're still confirming product-market fit.

For a lot of companies, neither is right.

**SAFEs work well early, but they come with trade-offs.** SAFE holders arguably don't start the QSBS clock at investment. While SAFEs are outstanding, the cap table remains ambiguous: dilution, pro rata rights, and liquidation waterfalls are all deferred. SAFE stacking at different valuation caps creates conversion surprises that founders don't see coming. And because SAFEs defer governance entirely, founders who would benefit from an engaged board and investors who would benefit from protective provisions are left without either until the next round.

**The full NVCA suite is the gold standard, but it's built for Series A and beyond.** Five documents, a month to close, and legal fees that routinely exceed $50,000, built for companies that have already confirmed product-market fit, not companies still finding it.

## What this is

Three documents. Real preferred stock on your cap table from day one. A board with designated seats. Clear investor rights, protective provisions, and pro rata participation. QSBS eligibility from the start. A closing timeline measured in days, not months, at a cost closer to a SAFE round than a Series A.

The documents are built on the current NVCA model legal documents. The Certificate of Incorporation follows the NVCA model charter article for article and section for section. The Investment Agreement combines the functions of the NVCA Stock Purchase Agreement, Investors' Rights Agreement, Right of First Refusal and Co-Sale Agreement, and Voting Agreement in one document, in the NVCA order, so that counsel in a later round can map each provision to its NVCA counterpart on sight. The project began as a fork of the [Series Seed](https://github.com/seriesseed/equity) v3.2 documents and keeps their design goal: the smallest set of documents that puts a real equity financing in place.

## Why it matters

**For founders:**

- **Cap table certainty.** With priced equity, the cap table is clean and final at closing. No stacked SAFEs at different caps, no conversion surprises at the next round. Outstanding SAFEs and notes convert at closing on their own terms, into a separate sub-series if they convert at a lower price, so each investor's preference matches what they paid.
- **A functional board.** SAFEs defer governance entirely. Series Sane gives you designated board seats (founder, investor, and mutual designees) from day one.
- **One page of blanks.** Every deal-specific term lives in a Deal Terms table at the front of the Investment Agreement, on the Schedule of Purchasers, or in the Disclosure Schedule. The body of the agreement is never edited.
- **Series A readiness.** NVCA-aligned documents mean next-round counsel can redline Series Sane against the NVCA model and immediately see the delta.

**For investors:**

- **QSBS from closing.** Preferred stock purchased at a priced round starts the Section 1202 clock at closing. The Company represents its eligibility and covenants to maintain it.
- **Cap table clarity.** Every investor knows exactly what they own and where they stand.
- **Governance rights.** Board representation, information and inspection rights, a participation right in future rounds, a right of first refusal on founder transfers, and consent rights over the actions that matter.
- **Anti-dilution and next-round protection.** Broad-based weighted average anti-dilution in the NVCA form. If the next round's investors receive rights that are granted to all investors in that round, Series Seed holders receive the same rights; rights that depend on an investment threshold are available to Series Seed holders who meet it, counting their Series Seed investment.

## How these relate to the NVCA model documents

| NVCA Document | Series Sane Equivalent |
|---------------|------------------------|
| Stock Purchase Agreement | Investment Agreement, Articles 2 (purchase and sale), 3 (Company representations), 4 (Purchaser representations) and 8 (general provisions) |
| Investors' Rights Agreement | Investment Agreement, Article 5 (information rights, participation right, next-financing rights, QSBS) |
| Right of First Refusal and Co-Sale Agreement | Investment Agreement, Article 6 (securities-law restrictions, Key Holder right of first refusal, market stand-off); no co-sale right |
| Voting Agreement | Investment Agreement, Article 7 (board size and composition, drag-along, proxy) |
| Certificate of Incorporation | Certificate of Incorporation, same article and section numbering as the NVCA model |

Section 5.5 of the Investment Agreement provides for the transition to a full NVCA suite in the next financing and says exactly which rights carry over.

## The seed-stage choices

These documents include the parts of the NVCA suite that matter at seed and leave out the parts that don't. Everything in the second list is a Series A matter and comes in through the next-financing clause if the next round's investors receive it.

**Included:** 1x non-participating liquidation preference; broad-based weighted average anti-dilution; protective provisions over a sale, a charter amendment, senior stock, authorized share changes, dividends and redemptions, and board size; class election of directors; mandatory conversion on an IPO or a majority vote; an asset-sale redemption so a sale of the business without a dissolution still pays the preference; escrow and earn-out allocation rules; officer exculpation and an exclusive forum; information, inspection and participation rights for Major Purchasers; a Company right of first refusal on founder transfers, assignable to Major Purchasers; a drag-along with the NVCA investor protections; an irrevocable proxy; conversion mechanics for SAFEs and notes, with an optional Series Seed-1 sub-series for instruments converting below the round price; QSBS representation and covenant; sanctions and Rule 506(d) representations; a spousal consent.

**Left out:** co-sale rights; registration rights; redemption rights; pay-to-play; tranched closings; closing conditions and legal opinions (the documents sign and close at once); the Investor Director approval list; the governance policy suite; a Section 220 waiver; the CFIUS, outbound investment and data security representations; a severance cap.

## How to use these documents

1. Agree on the **Term Sheet** with your lead investor.
2. Fill in the Deal Terms table in Section 1.1 of the **Investment Agreement**, then the Schedule of Purchasers and Key Holders (Exhibit A) and the Disclosure Schedule (Exhibit C).
3. Fill in the **Certificate of Incorporation** blanks (company name, registered agent, authorized shares, Original Issue Price, director counts). Delete the Series Seed-1 language if no SAFEs or notes are converting below the round price, and Article Fourteenth unless the company is a quasi-California corporation.
4. Use the **Variable Reference Map** to confirm the numbers that appear in more than one document match.
5. Delete the drafting notes, circulate, sign, file the Certificate with Delaware, and close.

## Changelog

### October 2026

The documents were rebuilt on the current NVCA model documents (Certificate of Incorporation dated October 1, 2025; Stock Purchase Agreement dated October 28, 2025; Investors' Rights Agreement dated October 1, 2025; Voting Agreement updated June 2026; Right of First Refusal and Co-Sale Agreement updated April 2026).

Certificate of Incorporation

- Restructured to the NVCA model's article and section order, so that Section 2.3.1 is the Deemed Liquidation Event definition, 3.3 the protective provisions, 4.4 anti-dilution and 5 mandatory conversion, as in the model. The April 2026 draft's headings and cross-references did not match each other; they now do.
- Added broad-based weighted average anti-dilution (Section 4.4) as a self-contained section that can be deleted with one conforming change.
- Added an optional Series Seed-1 Preferred Stock for SAFEs and notes converting below the round price.
- Added the NVCA asset-sale redemption mechanic and escrow and contingent consideration allocation rule, and the October 2025 clarifications on statutory conversions, domestications and equity financings.
- Added officer exculpation, an exclusive forum provision with a federal forum for Securities Act claims, and severability.
- Replaced the model's repeated adjustment parentheticals with a single sentence (from the Cooley Series Seed fork), made the charter self-contained, and replaced share-count thresholds with a fixed 25% test.

Investment Agreement

- Replaced the agreement-in-an-exhibit structure with a conventional agreement: a Deal Terms table in Section 1.1 holding every variable, followed by Articles 2 through 8 in NVCA order.
- Hardwired Delaware law and Delaware courts.
- Rewrote the next-financing clause (Section 5.5) so that later-round counsel do not have to negotiate what it covers. Rights granted to all next-round investors flow to the Series Seed holders; threshold-based rights flow only to holders who meet the threshold, counting their Series Seed shares; economic terms, named-investor rights and side-letter rights do not flow. The prior language under which every Major Purchaser automatically remained a Major Investor in the next round is gone.
- Stated the Key Holder right of first refusal in the agreement, with the Company first and the Major Purchasers second, in place of a covenant to "obtain and maintain" one elsewhere.
- Added a Board size covenant, removal and vacancy mechanics, a vote to increase authorized Common Stock, specific enforcement, joinder of new 1% holders, and the NVCA drag-along conditions in full.
- Expanded the SAFE and note conversion mechanics, added short representations on taxes, financial statements and QSBS eligibility, and updated the sanctions and foreign person representations.
- Added a Key Holder closing deliverable for vesting and a spousal consent (Exhibit D).
- Fixed undefined terms, inconsistent names and other defects in the prior draft.

Term Sheet and Variable Reference Map conformed to the above.

### April 2026

Initial release as a fork of Series Seed v3.2, with the Most Favored Nation clause, Rule 506(d) representations, the convertible securities consent mechanism, and the Variable Reference Map.
